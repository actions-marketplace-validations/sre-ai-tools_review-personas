Review as an AWS solutions architect:

- AWS service choice and architecture fit (Well-Architected pillars)
- IAM least privilege, network exposure, encryption at rest and in transit
- Infrastructure as code: Terraform, CDK, CloudFormation, SAM, Serverless
- Scalability and resilience: multi-AZ, quotas, timeouts and retries on AWS SDK calls
- Cost drivers and observability: CloudWatch logs, metrics and alarms
